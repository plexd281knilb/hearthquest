## 2. Tiered Family Rules Engine

HearthQuest implements a 3-tier rules hierarchy that allows mixed-age family tables to play together:

### Tier 1: Storybook Mode (Ages 5–8)
- **Stats:** 3 Core Attributes:
  - **Brawn** (Strength, Athletics, Toughness)
  - **Brains** (Cleverness, Magic, Noticing things)
  - **Charm** (Persuasion, Animal friendship, Cheerfulness)
- **Dice:** d6 only.
  - Normal roll: Roll 1d6. **Success on 4, 5, or 6.**
  - Advantage / Helper: Roll 2d6, take highest.
- **Health:** 3 Hearts (Damage loses 1 Heart; snacks/rest restore 1 Heart).
- **Cards:** Actions are physical or digital cards ("Cast Sparkle Magic", "Shield Bash", "Bribe with Apple").

### Tier 2: Apprentice Mode (Ages 8–12)
- **Stats:** Standard 6 Abilities (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA) with simplified modifiers (+1, +2, +3, +4).
- **Dice:** Full polyhedral dice set (d20 for checks/attacks, d4/d6/d8/d10/d12 for effects).
- **Mechanics:** Advantage/Disadvantage, Armor Class (AC), simplified spell slots (Tier 1/2/3 spells), and non-lethal knockouts.
- **Visuals:** Color-coded action cards highlighting which die to roll.

### Tier 3: Standard 5e (Teens & Adults)
- Full SRD 5.1 ruleset: proficiencies, feats, class progressions (Fighter, Wizard, Rogue, Cleric, etc.), spell slots, and condition tracking.

## 3. Dynamic Anti-TPK Math & Combat Scaling
- **The "Heroic Rally" Heuristic:** When any player falls below 20% HP or 1 Heart:
  - Enemy tactics shift from lethal to tactical (taking prisoners, gloating, aiming at scenery).
  - Kids gain an "Inspiration Point" or situational advantage.
- **Kid-Friendly Failure:** "Failing Forward"—a failed lockpick or stealth check doesn't halt the adventure; it introduces comedic complications (e.g. an alarm rings, a stray goat wanders into the room).
